Virtualisation Solutions

Unleash the full potential of your server hardware, reducing physical datacenter footprint and energy overhead by up to 60%.

Enterprise Hypervisor Deployment

We design and deploy VMware vSphere and Cisco HyperFlex architectures. Physical servers are consolidated into logical resources pool, facilitating dynamic CPU, memory, and networking allocations.

  • Dynamic resource load balancing prevents database execution bottle-necks.
  • Zero-downtime virtual machine migrations (vMotion) for live hardware upgrades.
  • Support for private, public, and hybrid cloud integration.

Operational Resiliency

Business Continuity & Disaster Recovery

Ensure your company can recover immediately from fire, ransomware, or catastrophic system failure.

Veeam Cloud Replication

We implement automated backups following the 3-2-1 backup rule: three copies of data, across two distinct storage mediums, with one offsite cloud copy.

  • Ransomware-proof immutable backups that cannot be modified or deleted.
  • Recovery Time Objective (RTO) under 15 minutes for critical database instances.
  • Continuous replica synchronization with regional data centers.
Disaster Recovery Risk

A physical database server crashes. Legacy tape backups are either corrupted or take 3-5 days to compile and rebuild.

Naledzi BCDR Strategy: Implement live virtualization snapshots replicated to offsite cloud nodes. In a crash, failover VM nodes spin up instantly, maintaining operations.
